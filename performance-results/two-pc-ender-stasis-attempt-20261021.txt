# Two-PC ender pearl stasis probe — inconclusive — 2026-10-21

## Scope and artifacts

Disposable two-PC pair using the current Paperclip SHA-256 `3D046088BE2610B19F3B64A99496BBEB05FA52ED8660D46D7762F47307D61D3D` on both PCs and Master SHA-256 `BD112490769F0B0037D4772CF02C88317B8F38F504579F1A66B1A73049DD6968`; Temurin JRE 25.0.4.1. PC1 ran Master/Backend A, PC2 ran Backend B. The player logged in through the isolated built-in proxy. SSH used the saved host keys with strict checking. No production world, service, or Crafty access.

## Attempt

Backend B owned the force-loaded test chunk. It contained a soul-sand bubble column from y=61 through y=89 and a waterlogged oak trapdoor at y=90; Backend B's RCON conditional checks confirmed `bubble_column` at `(10005,89,10005)` and a waterlogged trapdoor at `(10005,90,10005)`. A survival protocol client received water breathing, stood at `(10005.5,60,10009.5)` facing yaw -180/pitch -35, received one ender pearl, and sent the normal `use_item` packet.

The pearl entity spawned. Sequential RCON samples briefly found the same UUID on both backends, but the recorded positions differed by about 3.4 blocks in Z while it was moving. A later query found no pearl and the player had been displaced below the test floor. The sample timing and aim did not establish whether the pearl entered the intended holding position. The run does not verify stasis or stable cross-backend position and does not establish a MultiPaper defect.

An earlier throw with a solid glass cap teleported the player into water and caused drowning; that geometry was invalid for a stasis hold. The player was protected with water breathing for the waterlogged-trapdoor attempt. Both disposable backends saved and stopped cleanly, followed by the Master (`Exiting safely`); all private test ports closed.

## Disposition

INCONCLUSIVE. Do not count this as a stasis pass or failure. Ender pearl stasis remains a release gate. A follow-up needs to confirm the client trajectory actually enters the bubble column and sample the pearl while stationary before assessing replication.
