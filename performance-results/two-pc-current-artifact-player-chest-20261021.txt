# Two physical PCs: player-driven remote chest transaction — 2026-10-21

## Result

PASS. An offline 26.3 protocol client connected through the isolated Master built-in proxy on PC1, was routed to Backend A, and opened a chest owned by Backend B. The existing `scripts/Run-PlayerContainerClickProbe.js` performed ordinary normal clicks through the chest GUI.

- Backend B started with a fresh disposable world and generated/loaded chunk `[375, 375]` (block coordinate `6005, 6005`) before the chest was placed.
- Backend A did not load that test chunk before the client teleported there. While the client was online after the transfer, Backend A's RCON returned `That position is not loaded` for the chest; Backend B returned chest block data and an empty `Items` list.
- Client output: `WINDOW_OPEN`, clicks on chest slot 0 and player slot 27, then `PASS: normal chest GUI clicks moved exactly 16 diamonds into player inventory slot 9.`
- While the player was connected, both Backend A and Backend B returned `[{Slot: 9b, id: "minecraft:diamond", count: 16}]` for the player's inventory. Backend B returned `Items: []` for the source chest. This conserves all 16 diamonds across a physical-PC boundary.
- `save-all flush` succeeded on both backends. The isolated backends stopped, and the Master logged `Exiting safely`; the test listeners closed.

## Limits

This verifies one normal chest click transfer across the two physical PCs and a synchronized online player inventory. It does not test a backend or Master crash during the open transaction, a disconnect before save, shulkers/chests with concurrent users, online-mode Mojang authentication, or sustained survival play. It is not a complete item conservation or public-SMP safety certification.
