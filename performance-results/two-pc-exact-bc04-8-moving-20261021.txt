Warnings and limits
-------------------
Backend A logged two Mojang session-profile HTTP read timeouts during offline-mode synthetic sessions. Backend B logged five `RequestEntityPacket` warnings for entity UUIDs it could not find on Backend A. No test client disconnected or errored, but the entity warnings are not classified by this probe and should not be counted as a clean gameplay result. The run contains no farms, redstone, container transactions, exploration, or crash recovery. It does not establish capacity beyond eight players or a safe maximum; retain eight as a conservative soft cap pending representative gameplay and warning classification.

Both backends completed `save-all flush` with zero clients online and stopped after their RegionFile I/O completed. The Master then logged `Exiting safely`; all fixture game, RCON, proxy, and Master listeners were confirmed closed on both PCs. The temporary runtime and world data remained confined to the unique disposable `/tmp` fixture paths.
