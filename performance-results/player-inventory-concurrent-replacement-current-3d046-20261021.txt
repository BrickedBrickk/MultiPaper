This confirms the stale-replacement guard on the exact current JAR pair for an adversarial concurrent admin mutation to mirrored player inventory. It does not model two players clicking simultaneously, cursor movement, crafting result clicks, crash recovery, or a disk failure. The test world was disposable and reused; this result is anti-duplication regression evidence, not a general public capacity or inventory-safety certification.
