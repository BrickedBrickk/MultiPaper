Peer handshake connection selection and adjacent chunk generation regression
Date: 2026-10-21
Environment: Disposable local fixture `MultiPaper-26.3-final-clean-smoke-20261021`; no production/Crafty access.

The earlier stall reproduction showed each backend accepted two simultaneous peer connections and rejected a later hello based on `System.nanoTime()`. The peer `nanoTime` values came from separate JVMs and therefore could not be safely compared to pick the shared TCP channel. Changed peer selection so both servers choose one connection direction from the lexicographically ordered server names: the first name initiates and the second accepts. The nonce comparison now only arbitrates connections from the same direction. Selection and assignment are synchronized per peer. Non-preferred and stale duplicate channels are closed.
